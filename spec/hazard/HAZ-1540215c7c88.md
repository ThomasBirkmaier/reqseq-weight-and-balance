title: Centre of gravity outside the envelope shown as within limits
rationale: A pilot who takes off with the centre of gravity outside the envelope can lose control of the aircraft, in particular with an aft centre of gravity.
severity: catastrophic
---

The system shows a loading as within limits although its centre of gravity at its total mass lies outside the envelope.
