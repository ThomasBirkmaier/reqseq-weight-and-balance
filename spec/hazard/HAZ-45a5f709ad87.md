title: Wrong fuel quantity or fuel data used for a limit indication
rationale: Release 2 takes the fuel quantity from the fuel system module of a supplier; the report can be old or wrong, and the fuel data comes through an interface data file.
severity: hazardous
---

The limit indication is calculated with a fuel quantity that differs from the usable fuel on board, or with a wrong fuel density or fuel lever arm, and shows within limits for an aircraft that is not within limits at take-off or at landing.
