status: draft
title: Invalid, missing or wrong data used for a limit indication
rationale: A missing entry, a mass above the maximum of a loading station, a negative value, a value that is not a number, or the empty mass of another aircraft would make the calculated total mass and centre of gravity wrong without the pilot noticing.
severity: hazardous
---

The system calculates and shows a limit indication from a loading in which an entry is missing or is not a valid mass for its loading station, or from the data of another aircraft than the one the pilot will fly.
