title: Know before take-off whether the landing is within limits
rationale: Burning fuel moves the centre of gravity; a loading within limits at take-off can be outside limits at landing. Asked for by the client in release 1 (check at landing) and deferred to release 2 by the scope decision.
priority: must
validation-criteria:
  - For each aircraft type, for three loadings with fuel and a planned fuel burn prepared by the instructor (one within limits at take-off and at landing, one within limits at take-off but outside at landing, one with the landing exactly on a limit), the pilot names the correct landing result from the screen without calculating; a loading exactly on a limit counts as within limits.
  - "The instructor confirms the landing results against the paper loading sheet. Needs real operation: not met in the pilot; a scenario walk-through stands in until the field validation with real people at a real aircraft."

The pilot needs to know before take-off whether the mass and the centre of gravity of the aircraft will still be within the permitted limits at landing, after the planned fuel burn.
