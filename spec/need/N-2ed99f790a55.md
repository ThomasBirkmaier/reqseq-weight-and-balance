title: Know at once whether the centre of gravity is permitted

priority: must
validation-criteria:
  - For each aircraft type of the fleet, for three loadings agreed with the instructor beforehand (one inside, one exactly on the edge and one outside the envelope), the pilot names the correct result from the screen in each case without calculating; a loading exactly on the edge counts as inside.
  - The instructor confirms the results against the paper loading sheet.

The pilot needs, before take-off, the certainty that the centre of gravity of the loaded aircraft is within the permitted limits, seen at once after entering the load.
