validation-criteria:
  - For each aircraft type of the fleet, for three loadings prepared by the instructor (one below, one exactly at and one above the maximum take-off mass) the pilot names the correct result from the screen in each case without calculating; a loading exactly at the maximum counts as permitted.
  - The pilot confirms that the result was visible without scrolling or pressing a button after the last entry.

The pilot needs, before take-off, the certainty that the mass of the loaded aircraft is within the permitted limit, seen at once after entering the load.
