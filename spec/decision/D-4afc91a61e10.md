title: Validation of person-only criteria in release 1
rationale: No real pilot, instructor or maintenance organisation exists for the invented aircraft; the validation method must be stated and the open field validation kept visible (IV&V validation assessment, client validation answer).
---

Some validation criteria of the needs of release 1 can only be met by a real person at a real aircraft: a pilot timed on their own phone, readability in daylight and in a dim hangar, flight mode on the pilot's phone, an instructor comparing with the released loading sheet, and the maintenance organisation comparing with its released data. The aircraft are invented and release 1 is not for flight operations, so no such person or released data exists in this project.

For release 1:

1. These criteria are validated by the flight school operator from the walk-through screenshots of each need (phone viewport, light and dark mode, offline, re-weighing) together with the automated tests that cover the same behaviour (contrast in light and dark mode, operation without network and with a slow network, result visible without scrolling, entries never covered by the result).
2. The "released loading sheet" and "released data" of the invented aircraft are the type data decisions [D-14600e](reqseq:8af32ef6-642a-4c83-9c76-b554fd14600e?version=n1:9a75306b3ec6e5a1) and [D-91084e](reqseq:153a5bdf-6759-4d0e-80d9-20b0c691084e?version=n1:362a6d6c8450bada); the envelope corners are shown as numbers in the app so that they can be compared.
3. A field validation with a pilot, an instructor and the maintenance organisation at the aircraft stays open and must be done before any real use. It also checks that the result stays visible with the phone's on-screen keyboard open.
4. Without network the app cannot know that newer aircraft data exists; it says so and shows the weighing date. Before real use (release 2) the app must show when the aircraft data was last updated.
