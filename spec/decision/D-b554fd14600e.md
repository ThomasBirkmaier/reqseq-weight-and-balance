title: Type data of the Halcyra HC-4
rationale: The aircraft is invented (the name Halcyra was checked not to be a real aircraft type). The values are plausible for a small four-seat single-engine aircraft. Type and fleet data stay in this decision and never in a system requirement, so that requirements stay valid when the data changes. A new weighing report changes this decision and the fleet data file of the app in one pull request.
---

The core product is built for a fleet of one invented aircraft type, the **Halcyra HC-4**, a four-seat single-engine aircraft. Datum: a vertical plane 1.300 m forward of the front face of the firewall. All lever arms in m aft of the datum.

| Item | Mass | Lever arm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front seats (pilot and passenger) | 0 to 230 kg | 2.000 m |
| Rear seats | 0 to 200 kg | 3.250 m |
| Baggage compartment | 0 to 50 kg | 3.950 m |
| Maximum take-off mass | 1150 kg | |

Aircraft of the fleet (invented identifiers, not registrations), from the current weighing report:

| Aircraft | Empty mass | Empty-mass lever arm | Weighing date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HC4-01 | 765 kg | 2.220 m | 2026-03-02 |
| HC4-02 | 778 kg | 2.236 m | 2026-09-14 |

Envelope (polygon, corners in order, boundary included):

| Corner | Total mass | Centre of gravity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 650 kg | 2.100 m |
| 2 | 850 kg | 2.100 m |
| 3 | 1150 kg | 2.300 m |
| 4 | 1150 kg | 2.470 m |

The front seats are forward of the empty-mass lever arm, the rear seats and the baggage compartment aft of it: two heavy front occupants without baggage reach the forward limit (for example 200 kg front seats: 965 kg at 2.174 m, forward limit 2.177 m), full rear seats with full baggage reach the aft limit.
