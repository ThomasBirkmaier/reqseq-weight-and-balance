title: Release 1 checks the loading without fuel
rationale: The commissioning frame of the project fixes the scope of release 1 without a fuel path; fuel comes in release 2 through a fuel system module of a supplier that tells the aircraft how much fuel is on board and where its mass acts. A simple fuel entry now would be replaced by that interface one release later. The client's safety argument (a result without fuel can look permitted while the aircraft with fuel is not) is accepted as valid; it is answered by the qualifier on the positive indication and by the scope notice, not by a partial fuel path. The client's goal is met only with release 2; this is stated openly in the validation of the need to enter what is on board.
---

Not in release 1, planned for release 2:

- Fuel as a loading station (client asked for it in round 1 and insisted in round 2 that fuel at take-off is part of what is on board).
- The check at landing after the planned fuel burn (client need M-1, priority must; becomes a need in release 2).
- A record of the check with aircraft, date, entries and result (client remark, priority could).

Consequences in release 1:

- The limit indication "Within limits" carries the qualifier "(without fuel)", so that no pilot reads it as a take-off result.
- The app shows the note "Fuel is not included in this version." next to the results.
- The app is not released for any flight, real or training (notice of the scope decision); the client stated it would not let anyone use release 1 for a real flight.
