title: Limit check rule
rationale: The flight manuals of comparable aircraft treat the limit lines as permitted. A single rule for every type keeps the check consistent.
---

A loading is within limits if and only if its total mass is at most the maximum take-off mass and the point (total mass, centre of gravity) lies inside the envelope or on its boundary. A loading with any invalid entry has no limit indication.
