title: Limit check rule
rationale: The flight manuals of comparable aircraft treat the limit lines as permitted. A single rule for every type keeps the check consistent.
---

A loading is within limits if and only if its total mass is at most the maximum take-off mass and the point (total mass, centre of gravity) lies inside the envelope or on its boundary. A loading in which any entry is missing (left empty) or invalid gets the limit indication "Check not complete" and never "Within limits (without fuel)". Each limit is evaluated on its own: the centre of gravity against the forward and aft limits of the envelope at the total mass (at the maximum take-off mass when the total mass is above it), the total mass against the maximum take-off mass.
