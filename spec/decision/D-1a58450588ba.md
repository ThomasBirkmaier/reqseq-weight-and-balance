The client confirms that the set covers what the flight school expects from the app.

Conditions of the confirmation:

1. A fuel burn larger than the usable fuel at take-off, usable fuel above the usable fuel capacity and a missing fuel figure each give "Check not complete" at take-off and at landing.
2. The notice "Invented aircraft. Not for flight operations." stays as long as the aircraft data is invented and the field validation is open.
3. No result says "without fuel"; the results name take-off and landing.
4. Release 2 is validated with the client again with screenshots, including the reported fuel quantity with its report time, the pilot's own figure replacing it, a landing outside limits after a take-off within limits, and offline use.

Validation criteria marked "Needs real operation" stay open until a field validation with real people at a real aircraft; a scenario walk-through with screenshots stands in.
