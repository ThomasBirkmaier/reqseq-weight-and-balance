title: Type data of the Halcyra HC-2
rationale: The second invented type is a two-seat trainer of the same family; it has no rear seats. In run 1 it is a ReqSeq variant of the core. Its envelope is deliberately tight aft; with two occupants nearly any baggage puts the centre of gravity aft of the limit, which makes it a useful training case.
---

The variant supports the invented **Halcyra HC-2**, a two-seat single-engine trainer. Datum: a vertical plane 1.300 m forward of the front face of the firewall. All lever arms in m aft of the datum.
