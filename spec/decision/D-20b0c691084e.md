The variant is built for a fleet of the invented **Halcyra HC-2**, a two-seat single-engine trainer of the same family without rear seats. Datum: a vertical plane 1.300 m forward of the front face of the firewall. All lever arms in m aft of the datum.

| Item | Mass | Lever arm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front seats (pilot and passenger) | 0 to 220 kg | 2.000 m |
| Baggage compartment | 0 to 40 kg | 2.900 m |
| Maximum take-off mass | 880 kg | |

Aircraft of the fleet (invented identifier, not a registration), from the current weighing report:

| Aircraft | Empty mass | Empty-mass lever arm | Weighing date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HC2-01 | 640 kg | 2.150 m | 2025-11-20 |

Envelope (polygon, corners in order, boundary included):

| Corner | Total mass | Centre of gravity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 550 kg | 2.050 m |
| 2 | 750 kg | 2.050 m |
| 3 | 880 kg | 2.130 m |
| 4 | 880 kg | 2.170 m |
| 5 | 550 kg | 2.170 m |

The envelope is deliberately narrow: two heavy front occupants without baggage reach the forward limit (220 kg: 860 kg at 2.112 m, forward limit 2.118 m), a light pilot with full baggage the aft limit (60 kg and 40 kg: 740 kg at 2.178 m), and full front seats with full baggage the maximum take-off mass (220 kg and 40 kg: 900 kg).
