- Release 1 has no fuel loading station: the usable fuel is not part of the loading.
- The Halcyra HC-4 is the core product; the Halcyra HC-2 is a variant that replaces the type data and excludes the rear seats.
- The system is a planning aid for training and demonstration and carries the notice "Invented aircraft. Not for flight operations."
