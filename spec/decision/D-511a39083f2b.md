- Release 1 has no fuel loading station: the usable fuel is not part of the loading.
- The Halcyra HC-4 is the core product. The Halcyra HC-2 is a variant: a separate build of the app for an HC-2 fleet, in which variant requirements replace the core requirements on the aircraft data and on the aircraft selection. Its missing rear seats follow from its type data, so no core requirement is excluded.
- The system is a planning aid for training and demonstration and carries the notice "Invented aircraft. Not for flight operations." and the statement "This app is an aid. The pilot in command remains responsible for mass and balance according to the flight manual."
