title: Units, input resolution and rounding
rationale: The loading sheets of the flight school use kilograms and metres. Whole kilograms are accurate enough for occupants and baggage; comparing unrounded values prevents a rounded display from hiding an exceedance.
---

- Masses are entered in whole kilograms (kg). A loading station with nothing on it is entered as 0 kg.
- Lever arms and the centre of gravity are in metres (m), moments in kilogram metres (kg·m).
- Displayed values are rounded half away from zero: masses to 1 kg, lever arms and the centre of gravity to 0.001 m, moments to 0.1 kg·m.
- Every limit check uses the unrounded values, computed exactly: masses in whole kg, lever arms in whole mm, moments in kg·mm as integers, and each comparison of a centre of gravity with a limit as a comparison of integer products without division. A loading exactly on a limit therefore compares as on the limit, independent of the order of the sum.
