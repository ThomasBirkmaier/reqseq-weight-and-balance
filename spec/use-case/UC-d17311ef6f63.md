title: Pre-flight loading check at the aircraft
rationale: Main usage scenario named by the goal of the client.
---

1. The pilot stands at the aircraft with a phone before the flight.
2. The pilot opens the app and selects the aircraft (identifier) to be flown.
3. The pilot enters the mass of the front seat occupants, the rear seat occupants and the baggage, and 0 kg for an empty loading station.
4. The app shows at once the total mass, the centre of gravity, the position in the envelope and the limit indication.
5. If the loading is outside limits, the pilot changes the loading (for example moves baggage) and checks again.

Context: daylight outdoors or dim hangar, one hand free, no reliable network.
