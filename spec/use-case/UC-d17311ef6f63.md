1. The pilot stands at the aircraft with a phone before the flight.
2. The pilot opens the app and selects the aircraft (identifier) to be flown.
3. The pilot enters the mass of the front seat occupants, the rear seat occupants and the baggage, and 0 kg for an empty loading station.
4. The pilot reads the fuel quantity reported by the fuel system module, compares it with the fuel seen in the tanks and replaces it with their own figure where it differs.
5. The pilot enters the planned fuel burn of the flight.
6. The app shows at once the total mass, the centre of gravity, the position in the envelope and the limit indication at take-off and at landing.
7. If the loading is outside limits, the pilot changes the loading (for example moves baggage or takes less fuel) and checks again.

Context: daylight outdoors or dim hangar, one hand free, no reliable network.
