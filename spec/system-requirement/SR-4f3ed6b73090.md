acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the HC-2 build is open, when the pilot selects HC2-01, then the app shows "HC2-01 · Halcyra HC-2" above the results.
  - Given the HC-2 build is open, when the pilot opens the aircraft selection, then it lists HC2-01 only.
  - Given the HC-2 build is opened, when no aircraft has been selected yet, then no aircraft is preselected, no mass entry and no result is shown, and the app shows "Select the aircraft".
