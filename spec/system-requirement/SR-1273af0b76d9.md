title: Loading table with moments

requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats and 20 kg baggage compartment, then the table shows the rows "Empty mass 765 kg 2.220 m 1698.3 kg·m", "Front seats 160 kg 2.300 m 368.0 kg·m" and "Total 945 kg 2.270 m 2145.3 kg·m".
  - Given the table of the first criterion, when the pilot reads below the table, then it states the rounding "Masses rounded to 1 kg, lever arms to 0.001 m, moments to 0.1 kg·m".

If every mass entry of the loading is valid, the system shall show a table with one row for the empty mass, one row for each loading station and one total row, each with mass in kg, lever arm in m and moment in kg·m rounded to 0.1 kg·m.
