acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats, 20 kg baggage compartment, 100 L usable fuel at take-off and 60 L planned fuel burn, then the table shows the rows "Empty mass 765 kg 2.220 m 1698.3 kg·m", "Front seats 160 kg 2.000 m 320.0 kg·m", "Rear seats 0 kg 3.250 m 0.0 kg·m", "Baggage compartment 20 kg 3.950 m 79.0 kg·m", "Usable fuel 100 L 72 kg 2.350 m 169.2 kg·m" and "Total at take-off 1017 kg 2.229 m 2266.5 kg·m".
  - Given the table of the first criterion, when the pilot reads below the table, then it states the rounding "Masses rounded to 1 kg, lever arms to 0.001 m, moments to 0.1 kg·m".

If every entry of the loading is valid, the system shall show a table with one row for the empty mass, one row for each loading station and one total row, each with mass in kg, lever arm in m and moment in kg·m.
