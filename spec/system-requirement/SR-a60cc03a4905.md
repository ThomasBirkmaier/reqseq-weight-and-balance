title: Indication within limits
rationale: The pilot needs a clear positive result; it is shown only when neither limit is exceeded, so that a loading outside limits is never shown as within limits.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats, 20 kg baggage compartment, 100 L usable fuel at take-off and 60 L planned fuel burn (1017 kg, 2.229 m), then the take-off limit indication shows "Within limits".
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 180 kg front seats, 160 kg rear seats and 45 kg baggage compartment (exactly 1150 kg, the maximum take-off mass), then the take-off limit indication shows "Within limits".
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 73 kg front seats, 200 kg rear seats and 47 kg baggage compartment (1085 kg, exactly 2.470 m on the aft limit), then the take-off limit indication shows "Within limits".

If a valid loading at take-off is within limits according to the limit check rule, the system shall show the take-off limit indication "Within limits".
