  - "Given the aircraft HC2-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 60 kg front seats and 40 kg baggage compartment (740 kg, 2.178 m, aft limit 2.170 m), then the take-off limit indication shows \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\"."
