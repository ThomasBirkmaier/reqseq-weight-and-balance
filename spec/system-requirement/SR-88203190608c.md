title: Limit indication at landing
rationale: "Burning fuel moves the centre of gravity: with the fuel lever arm forward of a loaded centre of gravity, the centre of gravity moves aft during the flight. The pilot must know before take-off whether the landing is within limits."
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats, 20 kg baggage compartment, 100 L usable fuel at take-off and 60 L planned fuel burn (landing 974 kg at 2.223 m), then the limit indication labelled "Landing" shows "Within limits".
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 60 kg front seats, 190 kg rear seats, 50 kg baggage compartment, 50 L usable fuel at take-off and 40 L planned fuel burn (take-off 1101 kg at 2.469 m within limits; landing 1072 kg at 2.472 m, aft limit 2.470 m), then the take-off limit indication shows \"Within limits\" and the landing limit indication shows \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\"."
  - "Given the aircraft HC2-01 is selected in the HC-2 build, when the pilot enters 60 kg front seats, 35 kg baggage compartment, 52 L usable fuel at take-off and 10 L planned fuel burn (take-off 772 kg at 2.1699 m; landing 765 kg at 2.1706 m, aft limit 2.170 m), then the take-off limit indication shows \"Within limits\" and the landing limit indication shows \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\"."
