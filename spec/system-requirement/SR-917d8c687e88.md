title: Indication of a centre of gravity outside the envelope
rationale: A centre of gravity outside the envelope must never be shown as permitted; the boundary counts as inside. The limits are taken at the total mass, so that a mass above the maximum take-off mass alone does not give this indication.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 70 kg front seats, 200 kg rear seats and 50 kg baggage compartment (1085 kg, 2.475 m, aft limit 2.470 m), then the take-off limit indication shows \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\" and does not show \"Within limits\"."
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 212 kg front seats, 6 kg rear seats and 8 kg baggage compartment (991 kg, 2.193 m, forward limit 2.194 m), then the take-off limit indication shows \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\" and does not show \"Within limits\"."
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 211 kg front seats, 6 kg rear seats and 8 kg baggage compartment (990 kg, exactly on the forward limit 2.19333 m), then the take-off limit indication does not show \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\"."

If the centre of gravity of a valid loading at take-off is forward of the forward limit or aft of the aft limit of the envelope at its total mass, the system shall show the take-off limit indication "Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope".
