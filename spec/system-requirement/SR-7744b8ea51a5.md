If every entry of the loading is valid, the system shall show a loading table with one row for the empty mass, one row for each loading station, one total row at take-off, one row for the usable fuel at landing and one total row at landing, each with mass in kg, lever arm in m and moment in kg·m, with the statement "Masses rounded to 1 kg, lever arms to 0.001 m, moments to 0.1 kg·m" below the table.
