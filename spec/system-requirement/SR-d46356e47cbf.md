Wrong fuel data gives a wrong limit indication. HC-2 replacement of SR-79804b: the statement of SR-79804b with acceptance criteria from the type data of the Halcyra HC-2, because ReqSeq has no validity per acceptance criterion (scope of release 3)."

If the fuel interface data file states a fuel density and a fuel lever arm for the aircraft type, the system shall add the usable fuel to the loading with the mass of its volume times that fuel density at that fuel lever arm.
