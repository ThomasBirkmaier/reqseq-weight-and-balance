title: No limit indication for an invalid loading
rationale: A limit indication calculated from an invalid loading could show within limits for an aircraft that is not within limits.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 in \"Front seats\" and 201 in \"Rear seats\", then the app shows \"Check not complete: correct the marked entries\" and does not show \"Within limits\"."
  - Given the loading of the first criterion, when the pilot changes "Rear seats" to 200, then the app shows a limit indication.

If at least one mass entry of the loading is invalid, the system shall show the message "Check not complete: correct the marked entries" in place of the limit indication.
