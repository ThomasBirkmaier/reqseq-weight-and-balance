title: Check not complete for a missing or invalid entry
rationale: A limit indication calculated from a loading with a forgotten or invalid entry could show within limits for an aircraft that is not within limits (client need never a wrong within limits).
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 201 kg rear seats and 0 kg baggage compartment, then the app shows \"Check not complete: fill in or correct the marked entries\" and does not show \"Within limits\"."
  - "Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats and 0 kg rear seats and leaves \"Baggage compartment\" empty, then the app shows \"Check not complete: fill in or correct the marked entries\" and does not show \"Within limits\"."
  - Given the loading of the first criterion, when the pilot changes "Rear seats" to 200, then the app shows a limit indication other than "Check not complete".

If at least one mass entry of the loading is empty or invalid, the system shall show the limit indication "Check not complete: fill in or correct the marked entries".
