title: Indication of a total mass below the envelope
rationale: A loading outside the polygon of the envelope is never within limits (client decision in release 3); release 2 compared a total mass below the lowest mass of the envelope with the limits at that mass and could show "Within limits". No aircraft of today's fleet data file can reach it, a new fleet data file can.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - key: b8d6
    text: "Given a fleet data file with an aircraft whose empty mass is 10 kg below the lowest mass of the envelope of its aircraft type, when the app is built from it and the pilot selects that aircraft with 0 kg in every loading station, 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, then the take-off limit indication shows \"Outside limits: total mass below the envelope\" and does not show \"Within limits\"."
  - key: cn28
    text: Given a fleet data file with an aircraft whose empty mass equals the lowest mass of the envelope of its aircraft type and whose empty-mass lever arm lies inside the envelope, when the app is built from it and the pilot selects that aircraft with 0 kg in every loading station, 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, then the take-off limit indication shows "Within limits".
  - key: spj9
    text: "Given a fleet data file with an aircraft whose empty mass is 10 kg below the lowest mass of the envelope of its aircraft type and whose empty-mass lever arm lies aft of the aft limit of the envelope at that mass, when the app is built from it and the pilot selects that aircraft with 0 kg in every loading station, 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, then the take-off limit indication shows \"Outside limits: total mass below the envelope\" followed by \"Outside limits: centre of gravity outside envelope\"."

If the total mass of a valid loading at take-off is below the lowest mass of the envelope, the system shall show the take-off limit indication "Outside limits: total mass below the envelope" ahead of an indication of the centre of gravity.
