title: Top view of the loading
rationale: The pilot sees where the load is and which loading station to change.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats and 20 kg baggage compartment, then the top view shows the marks "Front seats 160 kg", "Rear seats 0 kg" and "Baggage compartment 20 kg" from front to back.
  - Given the loading of the first criterion, when the pilot changes "Baggage compartment" to 50, then the mark shows "Baggage compartment 50 kg".

If an aircraft is selected, the system shall show a top view of the aircraft with each loading station marked at its place and labelled with its entered mass in kg.
