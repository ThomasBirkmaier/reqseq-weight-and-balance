title: Envelope chart with the loading point
rationale: The pilot sees how close the loading is to a limit and which limit (forward, aft or mass) is exceeded.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats and 20 kg baggage compartment, then the chart shows the envelope with the corners (650 kg, 2.100 m), (850 kg, 2.100 m), (1150 kg, 2.300 m), (1150 kg, 2.470 m), (650 kg, 2.470 m) and the point at (945 kg, 2.219 m) inside it.
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 70 kg front seats, 200 kg rear seats and 50 kg baggage compartment, then the chart shows the point at (1085 kg, 2.475 m) aft of the aft limit with the label "Aft limit exceeded".
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 200 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats and 0 kg baggage compartment, then the chart shows the point at (965 kg, 2.174 m) forward of the forward limit with the label "Forward limit exceeded".
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, then a table below the chart lists the five envelope corners in order with total mass and centre of gravity as numbers, from (650 kg, 2.100 m) to (650 kg, 2.470 m).

If every mass entry of the loading is valid, the system shall show a chart of the envelope of the selected aircraft type with the point of total mass and centre of gravity of the loading.
