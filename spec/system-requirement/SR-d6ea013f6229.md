title: Envelope chart with the loading points
rationale: The pilot sees how close the loading is to a limit and which limit (forward, aft or mass) is exceeded.
requirement-type: functional
acceptance-criteria:
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected, when the pilot enters 160 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats, 20 kg baggage compartment, 100 L usable fuel at take-off and 60 L planned fuel burn, then the chart shows the envelope with the corners (650 kg, 2.100 m), (850 kg, 2.100 m), (1150 kg, 2.300 m), (1150 kg, 2.470 m), (650 kg, 2.470 m), the take-off point at (1017 kg, 2.229 m) and the landing point at (974 kg, 2.223 m) inside it.
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 70 kg front seats, 200 kg rear seats and 50 kg baggage compartment, then the chart shows the take-off point at (1085 kg, 2.475 m) aft of the aft limit with the label "Aft limit exceeded".
  - Given the aircraft HC4-01 is selected with 0 L usable fuel at take-off and 0 L planned fuel burn, when the pilot enters 200 kg front seats, 0 kg rear seats and 0 kg baggage compartment, then the chart shows the take-off point at (965 kg, 2.174 m) forward of the forward limit with the label "Forward limit exceeded".

If every entry of the loading is valid, the system shall show a chart of the envelope of the selected aircraft type with the points of total mass and centre of gravity at take-off and at landing.
