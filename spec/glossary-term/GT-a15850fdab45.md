title: Envelope
definition: The closed polygon of permitted combinations of total mass (kg) and centre of gravity (m) for an aircraft type, including its boundary. Its forward limit and aft limit at a total mass are the smallest and the largest permitted centre of gravity at that mass; for a total mass above the maximum take-off mass they are those at the maximum take-off mass, and for a total mass below the lowest mass of the polygon those at that lowest mass, as the limit check rule compares them.
