title: Fleet data file
definition: "The file `data/fleet.csv` in which the maintenance organisation records the aircraft of the fleet, one line per aircraft: its identifier, its aircraft type, its empty mass in kg, its empty-mass lever arm in mm and its weighing date. Each build uses the aircraft of its aircraft type."
