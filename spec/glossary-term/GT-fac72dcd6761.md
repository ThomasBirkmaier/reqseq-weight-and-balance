title: Weighing date
definition: The date (YYYY-MM-DD) of the weighing report from which the empty mass and the empty-mass lever arm of an aircraft come, as recorded for that aircraft in the fleet data file. It is the date of the aircraft data that the offline notice names; the report time of a fuel report never counts toward it.
