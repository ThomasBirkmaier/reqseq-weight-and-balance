title: Limit indication
definition: "The statement the system shows for a loading at take-off and at landing, labelled \"Take-off\" and \"Landing\", one of three: \"Within limits\"; \"Outside limits\" with the limit that is exceeded, or two \"Outside limits\" statements, the mass first, when a mass limit and a centre of gravity limit are both exceeded; or \"Check not complete\" when an entry is missing or not a valid entry."
