title: Usable fuel
definition: The fuel in the tanks that the engine can use, in litres (L). Its mass in kg is its volume times the fuel density stated by the fuel system module, and it acts at the fuel lever arm stated by the fuel system module. Unusable fuel is part of the empty mass.
