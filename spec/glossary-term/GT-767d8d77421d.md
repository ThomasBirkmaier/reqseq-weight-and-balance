title: Valid entry
definition: "An entry of the loading that holds a whole number from 0 to the maximum of its loading station: in kg for a mass, in litres (L) for the usable fuel at take-off; a planned fuel burn is valid as a whole number of litres from 0 to the usable fuel at take-off. A valid loading is a loading in which every entry is valid."
